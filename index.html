Our Heritage

About Prem Shankar Murti Kala Kendra

Preserving generations of Indian pottery craftsmanship, devotional sculpturing, and local community service.

Crafting Devotion & Utility Since 1994

Founded by Prem Shankar Prajapati, our studio was established with a mission to preserve the sacred art of traditional clay sculpting and Plaster of Paris (POP) idol crafting. Over the decades, our workshop has sculpted thousands of idols of Lord Ganesha, Goddess Durga, Goddess Lakshmi, and Shri Ram Darbar sets.

Understanding local community needs, we provide healthy mitti cookware (₹80-₹180), God motifs (at most ₹200), flower vases, live nursery plants (₹40-₹150), and everyday cooking utilities like portable gas stoves and 5kg cylinders.

Our Guarantee:

Every clay item is kiln-fired for durability, every POP idol is hand-finished with meticulous devotion, and every gas stove/cylinder is pressure-checked for safety.
